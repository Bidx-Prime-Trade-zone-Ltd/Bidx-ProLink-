Privacy Policy

Bidx ProLink · Effective date: 8 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BIDX PRIME TRADE ZONE LTD (“Bidx”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, discloses and otherwise processes personal information when you access or use Bidx ProLink. We aim to handle personal information lawfully, fairly, transparently and for specified purposes, in accordance with applicable law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

1. Information we process

  • Account information: email address, authentication identifiers and account settings.
  • Personal and profile information: name, phone and WhatsApp numbers, date of birth, state, local government area, address, profession, skills and biography.
  • Professional information: business details, work experience, portfolio photographs and qualification or verification information you choose to submit.
  • Support information: enquiries, feedback and correspondence with our support team.
  • Technical information: limited device, browser, diagnostic and security information needed to operate, maintain and protect the service.

2. Identity information and documents

Professional onboarding may request a National Identification Number (NIN) and supporting identity or qualification documents for verification. These details are sensitive. In the current app implementation, selecting an identity-document file is a local selection only; it does not mean the file has been securely uploaded or received by Bidx. Do not send passwords or NIN details through ordinary support email. We will update this notice if secure document collection is introduced.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; display professional profiles; help customers discover professionals; support service requests and communications; review professional information; respond to enquiries; prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents; maintain and improve the platform; and comply with legal obligations.

4. Lawful grounds

Depending on the processing activity, we rely on steps taken at your request or performance of a contract, your consent, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, or compliance with legal obligations. Where consent is the basis, you may withdraw it, subject to legal limits and processing already carried out.

5. Profile visibility and disclosure

Information you choose to include in a professional profile may be visible to other users. We may disclose necessary information to infrastructure, hosting, authentication, security, communications and other service providers acting on our instructions, or where disclosure is required by law. We do not sell personal information. Private identity numbers and identity documents are not intended to appear in public profiles.

6. Service providers and international processing

Bidx ProLink uses third-party infrastructure providers, including Supabase for account services and Cloudflare for app hosting. These providers may process information in locations where they or their infrastructure operate. We take reasonable steps to use appropriate providers and safeguards in line with applicable law.

7. Security

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including service delivery, security, dispute resolution and legal obligations. Following a verified deletion request, we aim to delete or de-identify associated account information. Limited records may be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, disputes or legal claims. Access to retained records is restricted.

9. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, erasure, restriction or objection to certain processing, portability where applicable, and withdrawal of consent. You may also lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. To exercise a right, contact Support@bidxpro.online.

10. Adults only

Bidx ProLink is strictly for people aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly permit anyone under 18 to create an account or use the service. If we become aware that an account belongs to a person under 18, we will take appropriate steps to restrict the account and handle associated information in accordance with applicable law.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our service or applicable law changes. The effective date above will be revised when an updated policy is published. Material changes will be communicated through the app where reasonably practicable.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or data-rights requests, contact BIDX PRIME TRADE ZONE LTD at Support@bidxpro.online or Info@bidxpro.online.
