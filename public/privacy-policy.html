Privacy Policy

Bidx ProLink · Effective date: 18 November 2026

Effective date: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BIDX PRIME TRADE ZONE LTD (“Bidx”, “we”, “our” or “us”) collects, receives, uses, stores, protects, retains and shares personal information in connection with Bidx ProLink. Bidx ProLink is a professional discovery and connection platform that enables customers to find independent professionals and enables professionals to create profiles, present their skills and experience, and make their services discoverable. We are committed to handling personal information responsibly, transparently and for legitimate, clearly explained purposes. This Policy should be read together with our Terms and Conditions and ProLink Platform Policy.

1. Information we collect

The information we collect depends on how you use the platform, the features you choose to access and whether you register as a customer or as a professional. We seek to collect information that is reasonably relevant to providing, administering, protecting and improving the services. The categories may include:

  • Account and registration information: email address, account identifiers, sign-in and authentication information, account preferences, registration details and information used to maintain and secure your account.
  • Personal and contact information: full name or legal name, telephone number, WhatsApp contact number where supplied, date of birth, country, state, local government area, city, service location and other contact or location details you choose to provide.
  • Professional and business information: profession, trade or occupation, business or trading name, professional title, skills, areas of specialisation, years of experience, work history, service descriptions, service areas, availability, portfolio information and other details used to describe your professional services.
  • Identification and supporting documents: documents or images submitted during professional registration or a verification process, which may include a professional identification card, work or staff identification, trade or competency certificate, qualification records, licence or permit where relevant, business-related documentation, references, and other supporting credentials reasonably requested for the applicable review.
  • Profile and user-submitted content: profile photographs, business logos, portfolio images, descriptions, service information, reviews, ratings, feedback and other content you choose to upload, publish or submit through the platform.
  • Communications and support information: messages, enquiries, complaints, feedback, support requests, reports, attachments and the information you provide when communicating with our support team or responding to a review request.
  • Transaction and subscription information: subscription status, plan selection, payment confirmation, payment references, invoices or receipts and relevant transaction records. Payment card or other payment credentials are handled through the applicable payment process and are not requested for publication on your professional profile.
  • Technical, device and security information: information such as device and browser characteristics, app version, log records, approximate network information, diagnostic events and security signals, where made available through the app or supporting services and where reasonably required to operate, troubleshoot and protect the platform.

Please provide information that is accurate, current and relevant to the purpose for which it is requested. You should only submit documents or information that belong to you or that you are legally authorised to provide.

2. Why we collect and use your information

We do not collect personal information without a service-related purpose. Information is used for account administration, professional registration, documentation and credential review, platform operation and user protection. Depending on the feature and the information involved, we may use it to:

  • create, register, authenticate and administer your account, and help you securely access the platform;
  • confirm the details you provide during professional onboarding and maintain an accurate professional record;
  • receive, organise, assess and review professional identification cards, qualifications, trade certificates, licences where relevant and other supporting credentials submitted for documentation and verification purposes;
  • support manual assessment of submitted documents, communicate where a document is unclear or incomplete, request additional information where reasonably necessary, and record the outcome of a review;
  • create and maintain professional profiles so customers can discover professionals by trade, skill, service area and other relevant profile information;
  • display the profile information you choose to make public and enable customers and professionals to connect through available platform features;
  • provide customer support, respond to questions and complaints, investigate reports and communicate important account or service notices;
  • administer subscriptions, record payment confirmations and provide relevant account or billing support;
  • detect, prevent and investigate impersonation, fraudulent documents, misleading profiles, unauthorised access, abuse, spam and other activity that may compromise users or the platform;
  • maintain platform availability, troubleshoot technical issues, monitor system performance and improve the reliability, accessibility and functionality of ProLink;
  • protect the rights, safety, property and legitimate interests of users and Bidx, and comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

Document information is collected for professional registration, record-keeping and review purposes. It is not collected for the purpose of displaying private documents publicly or making them available to other users as part of an ordinary professional profile.

3. Professional documentation and review

Some professional features require applicants to provide documentation or supporting credentials. The documents requested may differ according to the profession, service category, country, the nature of the application and the checks reasonably required for that application. Examples may include professional identification cards, work identification, trade certificates, training or qualification records, relevant licences or permits, business credentials and other documents that help explain or support the professional information submitted.

We use these materials to support administrative review, assess whether the information submitted is sufficiently clear and relevant, identify missing or inconsistent documentation, communicate with the applicant and record a review decision. A request for documentation does not mean that every applicant must provide one identical document. The documents required may vary depending on the circumstances and the professional category.

Access to submitted documentation is intended to be restricted to authorised personnel and service providers who need access to perform relevant platform, review, security or support functions. We do not intend to publish private identification documents, document numbers, private credentials or the contents of verification files on public profiles. A verification or approval status reflects only the review carried out by Bidx for the submitted information. It is not a government endorsement, a guarantee that a document is genuine in every respect, a professional licence, or a guarantee of a person's conduct, competence or future work.

4. Public profile information and private information

Bidx ProLink distinguishes between information intended to appear on a professional profile and information supplied privately for account administration, documentation review or support. Your public profile may include details such as your display name, profession, skills, experience, service description, service area, portfolio content and other information that the app identifies as profile information.

Documents submitted for review, account authentication details, private correspondence, security information and other non-public account records are not intended to be displayed as ordinary public profile content. Before submitting information, review the relevant screen and profile settings so that you understand what you are choosing to publish. Do not upload passwords, payment credentials, confidential customer records or another person's private information as profile content.

5. Legal grounds for processing

We process personal information where an appropriate legal basis applies. Depending on the circumstances, this may include taking steps at your request before entering into or performing an agreement with you; our legitimate interests in operating, administering, securing and improving the platform, provided those interests do not override your rights; your consent where we request it; and compliance with legal obligations. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it, although withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out lawfully before the withdrawal and may mean that a feature requiring the information can no longer be provided.

6. Third-party service providers

To make Bidx ProLink available and maintain its features, we use third-party service providers that perform services on our behalf. Our current core infrastructure includes Supabase for account authentication, database services and private file storage, and Cloudflare for application hosting, Worker/API delivery and related infrastructure. Depending on the feature being used or introduced, service providers may also support secure file delivery, communications, technical monitoring, security, customer support or payment processing.

Where a third-party provider processes personal information for us, it is expected to do so only for the services it has been engaged to provide and subject to appropriate contractual, confidentiality and security obligations. We do not authorise service providers to use personal information entrusted to them for unrelated purposes of their own. Some providers may process or store information in countries other than the country in which you live. Where information is transferred internationally, we will take steps required by applicable data-protection law to ensure that the transfer is appropriately protected.

We may also disclose information where disclosure is necessary to comply with a lawful request, court order or legal obligation; to establish, exercise or defend legal claims; to investigate suspected fraud or security incidents; or to protect users, the public or the rights and safety of Bidx. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

7. Data security and confidentiality

We apply reasonable technical, organisational and administrative safeguards designed to protect personal information against accidental loss, destruction, alteration, unauthorised access, misuse or disclosure. These safeguards may include access restrictions, authentication controls, secure transmission methods, role-based access, system monitoring and internal procedures for handling documents and support records.

Access to personal information should be limited to people and service providers who require it for an authorised business or service purpose. We expect personnel handling user information to treat it as confidential. However, no website, mobile application, electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect their account credentials, use devices responsibly and notify us promptly if they suspect that an account or information has been accessed without permission.

8. Retention of information

We retain personal information for no longer than is reasonably necessary for the purposes explained in this Policy, taking into account the nature of the information, the reason it was collected, the duration of the user relationship, the need to maintain professional and review records, security requirements, dispute resolution and applicable legal obligations.

Different categories of information may be retained for different periods. For example, account details may be retained while an account remains active; professional profile and review records may be retained while needed to administer the professional service and its integrity; and support, security or transaction records may be retained for an appropriate period to resolve issues, prevent abuse or meet legal requirements. When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete it, securely dispose of it or irreversibly de-identify it, subject to lawful retention requirements and legitimate reasons for retaining restricted records.

9. Your privacy rights and choices

Subject to applicable law and any lawful limitations, you may have the right to request confirmation of whether we process your personal information, access a copy of it, correct inaccurate or incomplete details, request deletion, request restriction of certain processing, object to certain processing, receive information in a portable format where applicable, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

You may also have the right to raise a concern with the relevant data-protection authority. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, so that personal information is not disclosed or changed for the wrong person. Some information may need to be retained where the law requires it or where retention is reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, disputes or legal claims. To request deletion of your Bidx ProLink account, use the Delete My Account option in the app or submit a request through our external account-deletion page. For other privacy requests, contact us using the details in the Contact section below.

10. Children's privacy and age requirement

Bidx ProLink is intended for individuals who are at least 18 years old. People under 18 are not permitted to create an account, use the platform or register as professionals. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children. If we become aware that an account has been created by a person under 18, we may restrict the account and take appropriate steps to address and securely handle the associated information in accordance with applicable law.

11. Links and services outside our control

The platform may contain links to websites, applications or services operated by other organisations. Those services are governed by their own privacy notices and practices. We encourage you to review the privacy information of any external service before providing personal information to it. This Policy explains Bidx's handling of information in connection with Bidx ProLink and does not govern services that we do not operate or control.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may revise this Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the platform, the information we process, our service providers, operational practices or applicable legal requirements. When we make a material change, we will take reasonable steps to make the updated Policy available through the app or another appropriate communication channel. The effective date at the beginning of this Policy indicates when the current version takes effect. We encourage users to review the Policy periodically.

13. Contact and privacy requests

If you have questions about this Policy, want to make a request concerning your personal information, or wish to report a privacy or information-security concern, contact Bidx Prime Trade Zone Ltd at Support@bidxpro.online or Info@bidxpro.online. Please describe your request clearly and avoid including passwords, payment credentials or unnecessary sensitive information in ordinary email correspondence.

14. Applicable data-protection law

We aim to handle personal information in accordance with applicable data-protection and privacy laws, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 where it applies. Nothing in this Policy is intended to remove or restrict a privacy right that cannot lawfully be excluded.
