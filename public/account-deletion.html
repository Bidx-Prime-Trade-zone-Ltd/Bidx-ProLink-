Bidx ProLink account deletion

This page allows you to request deletion of your Bidx ProLink account and associated personal data, even if you no longer have the app installed.

How it works

  1. Enter the email used for your Bidx ProLink account.
  2. Submit the request. Your email application opens with a prepared message to support.
  3. We verify account ownership before processing deletion.
  4. We confirm completion or explain any limited records retained by law or for security, fraud prevention or legal claims.

Support: Support@bidxpro.online
General: Info@bidxpro.online

The email is not sent until you press Send in your email app. Never include your password or NIN.
