Delete your Bidx ProLink account

This page is available to Bidx ProLink users who want to request deletion of their account and associated personal information, including users who have uninstalled the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Enter the email address registered to your Bidx ProLink account.
  2. Confirm that you are requesting permanent account deletion.
  3. Submit the request. Your email app will open with a prepared message addressed to our support team. Press Send in your email app to submit it.
  4. Our team will verify account ownership before processing the request. We may contact you if additional verification is needed.
  5. We will process the verified request and confirm the outcome. We may retain limited information where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, disputes or legal claims.
Important: This page submits a deletion request; it does not instantly delete your account. Account deletion is currently reviewed and processed by our support team after ownership verification.

Submit your request

If you cannot use the form, email Support@bidxpro.online from your registered account email. Do not include your password or NIN.
